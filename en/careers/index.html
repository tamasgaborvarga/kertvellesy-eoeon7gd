01

Lead architect

Employment type
Full-time and on-site only

Responsibilities

  • Leading large-scale architectural projects from concept to construction documents
  • Developing and implementing unique and innovative design strategies
  • Close collaboration with the project team
  • Liaising with clients; defining and managing their project requirements
  • Mentoring, developing and leading the architectural team
  • Project scheduling, budget management and quality assurance

Requirements

  • At least 5 years of experience in general design; applicants who have also worked in a leadership role are preferred
  • Outstanding design and visualisation skills, including 3D modelling and BIM (primarily ArchiCAD, Lumion, Twinmotion, etc.)
  • Proactive attitude, excellent organisational skills and the ability to meet deadlines
  • Excellent communication skills and the ability to coordinate teams
  • An innovative approach and commitment to sustainable design
  • BSc/MSc degree in architecture
  • Conversational English/German
  • Architectural design licence
  • Driving licence

What we offer

  • Premium leadership salary
  • Inspiring and creative working environment
  • Open studio life
  • Shared kitchen, coffee, fruit
  • Direct access to the Ciao restaurant + employee discount
  • Professional and personal development opportunities at national level
  • International contacts and partner meetings

Interested? Send your CV, cover letter and portfolio to karrier@kertvellesy.hu.

02

Architect

Employment type
Full-time or part-time / on-site or remote

Responsibilities

  • Taking part in large-scale architectural projects from concept to construction documents
  • Developing new and innovative design solutions
  • Close collaboration with the project team
  • Clarifying and documenting project requirements in meetings with clients
  • Supporting the work of the architectural team
  • Preparing and updating project documentation

Requirements

  • At least 3 years of experience in architectural design
  • A general designer’s mindset; the ability to see what matters
  • Experience in communicating with and managing subcontractors
  • Outstanding design and visualisation skills, including 3D modelling and BIM (primarily ArchiCAD, Twinmotion, Lumion)
  • Proactive attitude, excellent organisational skills and the ability to meet deadlines
  • Good communication and teamwork skills
  • An innovative approach and commitment to sustainable design
  • BSc/MSc degree in architecture
  • Conversational English/German
  • Driving licence

What we offer

  • Competitive salary
  • Inspiring and creative working environment
  • Open office, studio atmosphere
  • Shared kitchen, coffee, etc.
  • Option to work from home
  • Professional and personal development opportunities
  • International contacts and partner meetings

Interested? Send your CV, cover letter and portfolio to karrier@kertvellesy.hu.
