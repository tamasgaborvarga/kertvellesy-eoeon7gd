Gábor Kertvéllesy, architect

Hello. I am Gábor Kertvéllesy, architect. A born architect… My element is the feeling of life – as I like to say, I trade in it. Mostly, I give it.

Architecture is an opportunity to create a quality environment. And the architect has to create a feeling of life: the conductor of aesthetics and function together, guiding you.

Kertvéllesy Ingatlan Kft.

About the company

Kertvéllesy Ingatlan Kft. was founded in Pécs in 2005. Our design work ranges from simpler to luxury-standard residential buildings and apartment buildings. We design public and industrial buildings alike, and we also carry out partial or complete renovations of numerous listed and old industrial buildings.

Our aim is to run a practice of high professional and human standards that creates value.
