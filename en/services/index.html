Design

/ design with passion /

Design is a dialogue between us. The most important thing is that I understand you and your needs. The design is not about me; it is far more about you. I simply create the unity of colour, form, needs and circumstances.

Style itself is secondary – being stylish, however, is anything but. Harmony has to be felt and built, whether the mood is minimal, classic or Mediterranean. I enjoy my work; I love architecture. It gives me a world that is creative, entertaining and yet comprehensive enough. I would be glad to show it to you too…

03

Project management

/ your project, our duty /

In any investment – regardless of its size – the client’s goal is financial, technical and legal security, with deadlines properly met. This requires contracting and working with partners who have the right professional competence and attitude (e.g. contractor, supplier, site inspector, grant writer).

Project management is the support team that provides reassuring help from the moment the plans are handed over at the latest, up to the end of the warranty period if needed – whether it is reviewing the plans and the budget, selecting the right contractor, technical supervision or managing the entire process. Our goal is to deliver the most comfortable and most economical investment possible, tailored to your needs.
