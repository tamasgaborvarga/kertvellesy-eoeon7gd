The studio

How we work

I go through life with my eyes open; things interest me. I think about why things really work the way they do, and why people love what they love. This mindset is the foundation of my work: experience, and making it conscious.

Whether it is your home or your workplace, it matters that it says what it should. In the kitchen, the morning sun should fall on the cocoa; through the window, we should be able to wave to Dad washing the car. Your working environment should convey what you want to convey to your clients – refinement, warmth or persuasive power, as needed. People read the world of colours and forms without knowing it. I gently guide and attune them, so that it works better for you…

Design

Design is a dialogue between us. The most important thing is that I understand you and your needs. The design is not about me; it is far more about you. I simply create the unity of colour, form, needs and circumstances.

02

Interior design

Everyone longs for a special, innovative interior that offers a spatial experience yet stays functional, and for a bold, fresh exterior that is exciting and daring yet economical. Our mission is to deliver professionally and holistically considered projects for you.

Project management

In any investment – regardless of its size – the client’s goal is financial, technical and legal security, with deadlines properly met.
